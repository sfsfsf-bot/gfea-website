⚑ Official Domain Notice官方域名声明Aviso de dominio oficial

Golden Financial Exploration Association is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation in Washington State. We officially operate under the web domains gfea.one and gfeaone.org. We are currently in the process of transitioning our primary digital infrastructure to gfeaone.org. For all official organizational verifications, Google Workspace configurations, and external communications, please recognize gfeaone.org as our primary operational domain. Golden Financial Exploration Association owns and operates both domains. The legacy domain gfea.one is scheduled for retirement on December 31, 2026. This domain transition does not represent a change in organization, ownership, nonprofit status, or organizational leadership — both domains belong to the same legal entity, Golden Financial Exploration Association.
